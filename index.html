Your neighborhood garage

Straightforward car care in Suomenoja

Hannus autohuolto is an independent, full-service garage on Hannuksenpelto, serving drivers in Suomenoja, Matinkylä and the rest of southern Espoo. We handle everything from scheduled maintenance and brakes to AC service, diagnostics and exhaust work, on most makes and models.

No upselling, no surprises. We check the car, tell you what actually needs doing, quote the price before we start, and show you the old parts when we are done. That is how a local garage earns repeat customers.

It is November: winter tire season

Tire swap and tire hotel

Winter tires are required in Finland from December to February whenever conditions call for them, and studded tires are already allowed from November. November is the busiest tire month of the year, so book your slot now instead of queuing in the first snowfall.

  • Tire swap with balancing, from 35 €
  • Tire hotel: we store your off-season set, from 59 € per season
  • Free tread depth and pressure check with every swap
  • Same-day slots most weekdays
